📅 Day by Day

Full 11-Day Itinerary

Every link goes to the official website or ticket page. Click to plan and book!

🏰
Base 1 — Kraków
Royal capital · medieval old town · salt mines · castles & Eagle's Nests · July 14–17
3 Nights
Day 1 · July 14
Arrival + Historic Kraków
  • ✈️Land at KRK Airport — collect 2 rental cars (Hertz / Avis / Sixt)
  • 🏛️Rynek Główny — UNESCO Main Market Square; first dinner & evening atmosphere
  • 🏰Wawel Castle — Poland's royal castle on a limestone hill, sweeping city views
  • 🐉Dragon's Den (Smocza Jama) — fire-breathing dragon statue, activates every few minutes ⭐ Kids' Pick
Day 2 · July 15
Wieliczka Salt Mine + Kazimierz
  • ⛏️Wieliczka Salt Mine — UNESCO World Heritage; tunnels, chambers & stunning salt sculptures ⭐ Must-See
  • 🎟️Book tickets online in advance — they sell out fast in July!
  • 🕍Afternoon: Kazimierz (Jewish Quarter) — historic streets, art cafés & Plac Nowy market
Day 3 · July 16
Ojców National Park + Castle Trail
  • 🌿Ojców National Park — 16 km from Kraków; dramatic limestone cliffs, caves & easy family trails
  • 🏰Pieskowa Skała Castle — stunning Renaissance castle on a sheer limestone cliff above the river
  • ⚔️Ogrodzieniec Castle — dramatic Gothic-Renaissance ruins on the plateau's highest hill
Day 4 · July 17
Eagle's Nests Trail → Drive to Zakopane
  • 🦅Trail of the Eagle's Nests — 25 medieval castles & watchtowers built by King Kazimierz the Great
  • 🏯Ogrodzieniec Castle — most spectacular Gothic ruins in Poland ⭐ Highlight
  • 🏰Bobolice Castle — beautifully restored medieval fortress nearby
  • 🚗Evening: 1.5-hour drive south to Zakopane
🏔️
Base 2 — Zakopane & Tatra Mountains
Alpine lakes · valley hikes · river rafting · medieval castle · adventure activities · July 17–21
4 Nights
Day 5 · July 17
Arrive Zakopane + Gubałówka Hill
  • 🚡Gubałówka Hill — historic funicular from town centre, sweeping panorama of the entire Tatra range
  • 🛒Summer market & highland food stalls along the ridge
  • 🥩Evening: Krupówki Street — Zakopane's lively main promenade; try oscypek (smoked sheep cheese)
Day 6 · July 18
Morskie Oko — The Crown Jewel 🌊
  • 🏔️Tatra National Park — Poland's most beloved alpine park, small entry fee
  • 🌊Morskie Oko ("Eye of the Sea") — Poland's largest & most beautiful mountain lake ⭐ Unmissable
  • 🚶Paved 2-hour round-trip walk — or take a horse-drawn carriage up!
  • ❄️Turquoise water ringed by granite peaks and lingering snow — breathtaking
Day 7 · July 19
Dolina Kościeliska Valley + Caves
  • 🏞️Kościeliska Valley — easy trail through spectacular limestone scenery, streams & forest
  • 🦇Small caves & grottos along the route — peek inside with the kids
  • ⏱️3–4 hour relaxed walk, suitable for all ages; 20 min drive from Zakopane
  • ♨️Afternoon: relax in Zakopane's thermal pools
Day 8 · July 20
Pieniny: Dunajec Rafting + Niedzica Castle 🏰
  • 🚣Dunajec River Rafting (Flisacy) — 1–2 hr traditional raft through a dramatic gorge on the Polish-Slovak border ⭐ Kids' Pick
  • 🎟️Book via GetYourGuide — skip the queue, guaranteed spots
  • 🏰Niedzica Castle — 14th-century fortress above Czorsztyn Lake, legend of a lost Inca treasure
  • 🗺️~1.5 hours from Zakopane via Nowy Targ
Day 9 · July 21
Free Adventure Day + Drive to Kraków
  • 🧗Ropes course & zip-lines (Park Linowy) — tree-top trails for every age group
  • 🛷Gubałówka Summer Toboggan — long downhill slide the kids will want to ride again and again
  • 🌀Zorbing — giant inflatable ball rolling down a Tatra hillside
  • 🚗Evening: 1.5-hour drive back to Kraków
🏙️
Base 3 — Back to Kraków
Immersive museums · Jewish Quarter finale · last shopping · July 21–25
2 Nights
Day 10 · July 23
Womai Museum + Kazimierz Evening
  • 🔮Womai Immersive Museum — "In the Light" optics room & "In the Dark" total-darkness sensory journey ⭐ Ages 7–11
  • 🍽️Final dinner in Kazimierz — Kraków's most atmospheric neighbourhood for food & drinks
  • 🦁Option: Kraków Zoo — 1,400 animals from 300 species, hands-on petting area
Day 11 · July 25
Last Morning + Flight Home ✈️
  • 🛍️Morning shopping at Rynek Główny — Polish pottery, amber jewellery & linen
  • 🚗Return rental cars at KRK Airport
  • ✈️Direct flight back to Tel Aviv — take only memories 🇮🇱

💡 On the Ground

Practical Travel Tips

Everything two families need for a smooth and memorable trip through Poland.

📅
Book Everything Now
July is Poland's absolute peak. Book hotels, Wieliczka tickets and Dunajec rafting immediately. Popular slots go weeks in advance.
🚗
Two Rental Cars
Essential for two families. Book family SUVs (VW Tiguan / Skoda Kodiaq) via Hertz, Avis or Sixt at KRK Airport. Pre-order child seats for ages 7 & 11.
💰
Currency: Polish Złoty
Poland uses PLN (Złoty). €1 ≈ 4.3 PLN. Cards accepted almost everywhere. Carry cash for small stalls, national park fees & tips.
🌦️
July Weather
Kraków: 22–28°C, sunny & lovely. Tatras: afternoon showers are common — pack light rain jackets for everyone. Mountain mornings can be cool.
👟
Footwear Is Key
Bring proper hiking shoes for the whole family — needed for Tatra trails and Eagle's Nests castle hikes. Light trainers for city days.
🏔️
Tatra Park Entry Fee
Small fee per person at Tatra National Park entrance gates — cash or card. Worth every grosz for the scenery.
🛏️
Two-Family Rooms
Request connecting rooms in writing after online booking. Or rent a large apartment for 6–8 people — more space, shared kitchen, 30–40% cheaper.
🗺️
Useful Apps
Google Maps (download Poland offline) · Google Translate with Polish offline · AllTrails for Tatra hiking routes
