📅 Day by Day

Full 12-Day Itinerary

Every link goes to the official website or ticket page. Click to plan and book!

🏰
Base 1 — Kraków
Royal capital · Old Town · Salt Mine · National Park · July 14–16
3 Nights
Day 1 · July 14
Arrival + Historic Kraków
  • ✈️Land at KRK Airport — pickup 2 rental cars
  • 🏛️Rynek Główny (Main Market Square) — UNESCO listed, evening stroll & dinner
  • 🏰Wawel Castle & Cathedral — Poland's royal hilltop castle with city views
Day 2 · July 15
Wieliczka Salt Mine
🚗 20 min from Kraków
  • ⛏️Wieliczka Salt Mine — UNESCO World Heritage; tunnels, chambers & the stunning St. Kinga Chapel ⭐ Must-See
  • 🎟️Book Tourist Route tickets online — sell out fast in July!
  • 🌳Afternoon: Kraków parks & Old Town wandering
🔵 Waze: Wieliczka Salt Mine
Day 3 · July 16
Ojców National Park
🚗 40 min from Kraków
  • 🌿Ojców National Park — limestone cliffs, castles & easy family trails
  • 🦇Caves exploration (Łokietek Cave & Dark Cave)
  • 🏰Pieskowa Skała Castle — Renaissance castle perched on a limestone cliff
  • 🥾Family hike ~6–8 km round trip along the Prądnik river valley
🔵 Waze: Ojcowski Park Narodowy
🏔️
Base 2 — Zakopane
Tatra Mountains capital · hiking · alpine coasters · thermal pools · July 17–21
5 Nights
Day 4 · July 17
Drive to Zakopane + Town Exploration
🚗 ~2 hours from Kraków
  • 🏨Hotel check-in & settle in
  • 🛍️Krupówki Street — Zakopane's famous pedestrian promenade with food stalls & shops
  • 🚡Gubałówka Funicular — short ride to panoramic Tatra views, great for first evening
🔵 Waze: Zakopane
Day 5 · July 18
Morskie Oko — The Eye of the Sea
🚗 1 hour from Zakopane
  • 🏞️Morskie Oko Lake — Poland's most famous mountain lake, surrounded by peaks ⭐ Highlight
  • 🅿️Park at Palenica Białczańska — then walk or take horse carriage to the lake
  • 🥾~15 km round trip — paved road (flat), suitable for all ages
  • 🏔️Option: continue up to Czarny Staw for advanced hikers (+3 km)
🔵 Waze: Palenica Białczańska
Day 6 · July 19
Kościeliska Valley Hike
  • 🌊Kościeliska Valley — scenic river valley with bridges, caves & breathtaking mountain scenery
  • 🦇Mroźna Cave — easy tour with kids, impressive stalactites
  • 🥾~8–10 km easy family hike along the valley floor
  • 🧊Valley stays cool even in summer — bring a light layer
Day 7 · July 20
Adventure Day — Alpine Coaster & Rope Park
  • 🎢Zakopane Alpine Coaster — thrilling mountain ride, kids go absolutely wild ⭐ Kids' Pick
  • 🧗Linowy Park (Rope Park) — obstacle courses at multiple heights, great for ages 7+
  • 🏔️Mountain viewpoints & cable car rides for panoramic photos
  • 🍕Celebrate the day with dinner in Zakopane town center
Day 8 · July 21
Chochołowskie Termy — Water Park Day
🚗 25 min from Zakopane
  • 🌊Chochołowskie Termy — outdoor & indoor thermal pools with mountain backdrop ⭐ Best Water Day
  • 🎢Multiple water slides & pools for all ages
  • 🏊Full day of swimming, slides & relaxation — the only water-focused day
  • 💡Book tickets in advance: chocholowskietermy.pl
🔵 Waze: Chochołowskie Termy

3 Nights
Day 9 · July 22

  • 🌄Easy afternoon walk to enjoy the different mountain scenery of the Beskids

🚗 40 min from Szczyrk

  • 🏞️Jezioro Międzybrodzkie (mountain lake) — beautiful views & peaceful picnic spot
  • 🌅Spectacular scenic viewpoints overlooking the Żywiec reservoir

💡 On the Ground

Practical Travel Tips

Everything two families need for a smooth and memorable trip through Poland.

📅
Book Everything in Advance
July is Poland's absolute peak season. Book hotels AND Wieliczka Salt Mine tickets immediately. Popular slots sell out weeks ahead!
🚗
Two Rental Cars
Essential for two families. Book family SUVs (VW Tiguan / Skoda Kodiaq) via Hertz, Avis or Sixt at KRK Airport. Pre-order child seats for ages 7–11.
💰
Currency: Polish Złoty (PLN)
Poland uses PLN. €1 ≈ 4.3 PLN · $1 ≈ 4.0 PLN. Cards accepted almost everywhere. Carry some cash for national park fees & smaller stalls.
🌦️
July Weather
Kraków: 22–28°C, sunny. Tatras & Beskids: afternoon showers common — pack light rain jackets for everyone. Mountain mornings can be cool.
👟
Footwear Is Everything
Bring proper hiking shoes for the whole family. Needed for Morskie Oko, Kościeliska, Ojców & all mountain trails. Light trainers for city days.
🏔️
Tatra National Park Fee
Small entry fee at Tatra National Park gates — cash or card. Worth every grosz for the stunning scenery.
🛏️
Two-Family Rooms
Request connecting rooms in writing directly with hotels. Or rent a large apartment sleeping 6–8 — shared kitchen, more space, 30–40% cheaper.
🗺️
Useful Apps
Google Maps (download Poland offline) · Waze for mountain roads · AllTrails for Tatra & Beskid hiking routes
🎒 Gear Up

Packing Recommendations

What to pack for a mountain-focused family adventure in summer Poland.

🥾 Footwear & Clothing
  • Hiking shoes for everyone (waterproof recommended)
  • Light rain jackets for the whole family
  • Fleece or warm layer for mountain mornings
  • Comfortable trainers for city days
  • Swimwear (multiple sets — for Chochołowskie Termy day)
  • Sun hats and UV protection shirts
🎒 Backpacks & Gear
  • Day pack per adult for hikes (20–30L)
  • Trekking poles (optional but great for parents)
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+ for mountain UV
  • Insect repellent for forest trails
  • Reusable water bottles (2 per person)
  • Snacks & energy bars for long hikes
💊 Health & Safety
  • Basic first aid kit (plasters, antiseptic)
  • Blister prevention patches & foot cream
  • Kids' pain relief (ibuprofen/paracetamol)
  • Motion sickness tablets (winding mountain roads)
  • European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)
  • Travel insurance for mountain activities
📱 Tech & Documents
  • Passport & travel insurance docs
  • International driving license
  • Offline maps downloaded (Poland)
  • Power bank for long days out
  • Camera / action cam for alpine coasters
  • Universal EU plug adapters
